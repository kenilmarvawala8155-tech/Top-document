Pets are animals that people keep at home for companionship, entertainment, and care. Common pets include dogs, cats, parrots, fish, and rabbits.

Dogs are loyal and friendly animals that can guard homes and help humans in many ways. Cats are calm, playful, and independent pets that enjoy clean surroundings.

Parrots are colorful and intelligent birds that can imitate human speech, while fish are peaceful pets that live in aquariums and create a relaxing environment.

Rabbits are soft, gentle, and active animals that love eating vegetables and grass. Pets provide happiness, reduce stress, and teach people responsibility and kindness.

Proper food, shelter, cleanliness, and medical care are important to keep pets healthy and happy.Birds like parrots are loved for their colorful feathers and ability to imitate sounds and human speech. Fish are peaceful pets that live in aquariums and make homes look attractive and relaxing.
